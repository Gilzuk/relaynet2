MLP-169 is CONSTANT in M and L: 330 flops/symbol, 169 params.
Viterbi grows as M^L per symbol (states M^(L-1) x M branches); intractable for large M or L.
Honest caveat: at BPSK/L=3, Viterbi (64 flops/sym) is CHEAPER per-flop than the MLP (330 flops/sym); the MLP wins on scaling, not per-symbol cost here.
