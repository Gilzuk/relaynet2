\section{Appendix E: Response to Supervisor Comments}\label{sec:appendix-response-to-comments}

This appendix records every comment raised by the supervisor (\textbf{AK}) on the previous draft, and its resolution (\textbf{GZ}), condensed to a paraphrase of each. Each comment was treated as a required change: where something was identified as wrong or out of scope it was corrected or removed, and where it could not be acted on within the uncoded, single-relay scope of this work, that is stated plainly and carried into future work rather than argued against. \textbf{[Removed]} marks a resolution that deleted material rather than fixing it; the restructure around one canonical setup (Section~\ref{sec:introduction}) removed the Rician-robustness study, the $2\times2$ MIMO second hop and its ZF/LMMSE/SIC equalization, 16-PSK, the CSI-injection ablation, the end-to-end autoencoder, and the diversity--multiplexing-tradeoff and MIMO-capacity background passages, all driven by the single comment (Ch.~2, \#4) asking for one fixed model instead of several mixed together.
